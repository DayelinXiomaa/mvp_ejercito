# Prompts para la Generación de Recursos (IA)

Este archivo contiene un listado de prompts optimizados para generadores de imágenes (como Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Leonardo.ai o Stable Diffusion) para obtener los recursos gráficos del proyecto con un estilo profesional, coherente e institucional.

---

## 1. Mapa de Fondo (mapa_peru.jpg)
Para obtener un fondo de mapa con estilo táctico militar (similar al generado):

* **Prompt (Inglés - Recomendado)**:
  > A high-tech digital military tactical map of Peru, dark army green and deep charcoal grey background, glowing golden outline of Peru's borders, tech grid lines, radar scanning patterns, clean abstract sci-fi military dashboard layout, top-down map view, high contrast, ultra-detailed, 4k resolution, no text. --ar 1:1
* **Prompt (Español)**:
  > Un mapa táctico militar digital de alta tecnología del Perú, fondo verde militar oscuro y gris carbón profundo, contorno dorado brillante de las fronteras de Perú, líneas de cuadrícula tecnológica, patrones de escaneo de radar, diseño abstracto y limpio de panel militar de ciencia ficción, vista superior, alto contraste, ultra detallado, resolución 4k, sin texto.

## 2. Logotipos de las Escuelas de Armas (assets/logos/)
Para que todos los logos tengan una línea visual uniforme (estilo emblema táctico militar en relieve dorado y verde):

* **Estilo Base General**:
  > Military insignia emblem vector, gold metallic texture, dark olive green shield, modern sharp tactical design, isolated on black background, high resolution, 3D render feel, vector art style.
* **Detalles específicos por Arma**:
  * **Infantería**:
    > ...featuring two crossed rifles behind a central shield with a Peruvian flag color accent.
  * **Caballería**:
    > ...featuring two crossed sabers/swords behind a central horse silhouette in gold.
  * **Artillería**:
    > ...featuring two crossed historic cannons under a gold sun of May.
  * **Ingeniería**:
    > ...featuring a tactical castle fortress tower in the center with golden gears.
  * **Comunicaciones**:
    > ...featuring a globe of the earth with golden lightning bolts/radio waves wrapping around it.

## 3. Retratos de los Héroes (assets/heroes/)
Para lograr una galería con estilo visual uniforme (retratos al óleo de aspecto histórico y museístico, en vez de fotos modernas que desentonen):

* **Estilo Base General (Inglés)**:
  > Classic oil painting portrait of [Nombre del Héroe], 19th-century military hero, formal Peruvian army uniform with golden epaulets and brass buttons, proud historical posture, dramatic Rembrandt lighting, museum archive quality, highly detailed face, oil canvas texture, dark background. --ar 1:1

* **Prompts Específicos**:
  * **Mariscal Andrés Avelino Cáceres**:
    > Classic oil painting portrait of Andres Avelino Caceres, 19th-century Peruvian army general, wearing military uniform with golden epaulets, bald on top with thick dark side hair and a long full beard, intense heroic gaze, dramatic lighting, oil canvas texture, dark background.
  * **Coronel Alfonso Ugarte**:
    > Classic oil painting portrait of Alfonso Ugarte, young 19th-century Peruvian military hero, clean-shaven face with a thin mustache, wearing a white or blue formal Peruvian military uniform with gold trims, proud posture, oil canvas texture.
  * **Coronel Francisco Bolognesi**:
    > Classic oil painting portrait of Francisco Bolognesi, elderly 19th-century Peruvian army colonel, white hair and a white mustache, wearing a dark blue military uniform with high collar and golden epaulets, resolute expression, oil canvas texture.
  * **Teniente Coronel Pedro Ruiz Gallo**:
    > Classic oil painting portrait of Pedro Ruiz Gallo, 19th-century Peruvian military inventor, dark hair and mustache, wearing a military uniform, holding a drafting compass or standing next to gear machinery blueprints, oil canvas texture.
  * **José Olaya Balandra**:
    > Classic oil painting portrait of Jose Olaya Balandra, 19th-century Peruvian patriot, humble fisherman from Chorrillos, dark indigenous features, wearing a simple colonial white shirt, holding a secret letter behind his back, coastal dramatic sky background, oil canvas texture.

## 4. Prompts de Video para Google Veo / Google Omni

Estos prompts están optimizados para modelos de generación de video de Google (como Veo y Omni), enfocándose en movimientos de cámara estables, cinematografía militar épica y fidelidad histórica.

### A) Videos de Presentación de las Escuelas (assets/videos/)
*Recomendación de estilo: Ficción de acción militar, cinematografía moderna, 4k, cámara lenta (slow-motion), iluminación dramática.*

*   **Infantería (`infanteria.mp4`)**:
    > Cinematic high-action video of Peruvian infantry soldiers in modern tactical gear performing training maneuvers in a rugged mountain valley, running through dust in slow motion, low-angle camera tracking, intense and heroic atmosphere, 4k resolution, cinematic color grading.
*   **Caballería (`caballeria.mp4`)**:
    > Cinematic epic shot of modern armored tanks and military cavalry vehicles charging through a dry desert landscape, raising huge clouds of dust, drone shot panning down, powerful action movie aesthetics, cinematic lighting, ultra-realistic.
*   **Artillería (`artilleria.mp4`)**:
    > Dramatic military video of a heavy artillery battery firing at sunset, slow motion capture of the fire muzzle flash and smoke, camera shaking slightly on impact, high-contrast silhouette of soldiers operating the artillery cannon, 4k.
*   **Ingeniería (`ingenieria.mp4`)**:
    > Time-lapse and cinematic tracking shot of military combat engineers constructing a tactical pontoon bridge over a wide river, military trucks and heavy machinery in action, teamwork, high precision, bright daylight, realistic film.
*   **Comunicaciones (`comunicaciones.mp4`)**:
    > Sci-fi inspired military technology video, tactical communications antenna rising against a starry night sky, glowing abstract digital waves and signal lines overlaying the scene, close-up of a military operator looking at high-tech tactical screens, blue and green cyan lighting.

### B) Videos Homenaje a Héroes (`_homenaje.mp4`)
*Recomendación de estilo: Recreación histórica dramática, grano de película cinematográfica, atmósfera épica y solemne.*

*   **Andrés Avelino Cáceres (`caceres_homenaje.mp4`)**:
    > Historical cinematic reenactment of Andrés Avelino Cáceres standing on a misty Andean mountain peak, wearing a dark 19th-century general uniform with a long beard, holding a steel sword, wind blowing through his coat, looking resolutely into the horizon, fog rolling in, epic film grain.
*   **Alfonso Ugarte (`ugarte_homenaje.mp4`)**:
    > Dramatic slow-motion historical video of Alfonso Ugarte riding a majestic white horse, holding the red-and-white Peruvian flag high, charging at the edge of the high cliffs of Morro de Arica at sunset, heroic sacrifice scene, cinematic epic lighting, emotional and powerful.
*   **Francisco Bolognesi (`bolognesi_homenaje.mp4`)**:
    > Solemn historical film scene of Francisco Bolognesi in a candle-lit war room, surrounded by his officers, speaking with absolute determination, close-up of his face showing honor and courage, cinematic lighting, 19th-century details.
*   **Pedro Ruiz Gallo (`ruiz_gallo_homenaje.mp4`)**:
    > Cinematic scene of a 19th-century workshop, Pedro Ruiz Gallo looking at the gears and mechanism of a giant wooden clock, candlelight casting long shadows, close-up of brass gears turning in slow motion, intellectual and heroic mood.
*   **José Olaya Balandra (`olaya_homenaje.mp4`)**:
    > Dramatic night scene in Chorrillos bay, waves crashing, José Olaya swimming silently through the dark ocean water under moonlight, holding a sealed letter, looking back at the distant shore lights, high-contrast reflections on water, tense and heroic atmosphere.

## 5. Propuesta Estilo "Pantallas Holográficas Sci-Fi" (Estilo Avatar / Iron Man)

Si prefieres un diseño completamente futurista con estética holográfica de ciencia ficción (HUD militar futurista, tonos cianes, azul neón, wireframes 3D y elementos translúcidos de alta tecnología):

### A) El Mapa de Fondo (`mapa_peru.jpg`)
*   **Prompt (Inglés)**:
    > A 3D wireframe holographic map of Peru, glowing blue neon and bright cyan color palette, spinning radar grid lines, sci-fi military command center dashboard, digital analytics UI elements, transparent floating panels, volumetric light effects, dark futuristic background, ultra-detailed, 8k resolution.
*   **Visualización**: El mapa se convertirá en un plano digital translúcido tridimensional con líneas de relieve flotantes y rejillas de datos circulares en constante escaneo.

### B) Logotipos de las Escuelas (`assets/logos/`)
*   **Estilo Base (Inglés)**:
    > Futuristic sci-fi hologram military crest, semi-transparent glowing cyan vector emblem, glowing neon blue lines, floating HUD UI design element, isolated on black background, clean digital aesthetic.
*   **Variantes**:
    *   *Infantería*: "...featuring a stylized geometric combat helmet with glowing visor and crosshairs."
    *   *Caballería*: "...featuring a cybernetic robotic horse profile with neon vector armor lines."
    *   *Artillería*: "...featuring a heavy plasma railgun cannon schematic wireframe."
    *   *Ingeniería*: "...featuring a 3D isometric blue hologram blueprint of a high-tech modular fortress."
    *   *Comunicaciones*: "...featuring a satellite orbital network ring with holographic data signals emitting outward."

### C) Retratos de los Héroes (`assets/heroes/`)
*   **Estilo Base (Inglés)**:
    > Stylized sci-fi holographic portrait of [Nombre del Héroe], 3D blue-and-cyan glitching hologram face, digital grid projection lines, glowing neon circuit patterns on skin, semi-transparent HUD elements overlaying the portrait, futuristic military database profile look, dark background.
*   **Visualización**: Los retratos históricos de Cáceres, Bolognesi, etc., se verán proyectados como si fuesen transmisiones o perfiles digitales holográficos de IA táctica en tiempo real en la pantalla de control, con líneas de interferencia digital (glitch) y circuitos de neón.

### D) Videos Homenaje a Héroes (`_homenaje.mp4`)
*   **Estilo Base (Inglés)**:
    > Futuristic sci-fi video showing a floating 3D holographic reconstruction of [Nombre del Héroe] in motion, glowing blue digital particles assembling the character's form, rotating camera, database data scanning panels sliding across the screen, high-tech military laboratory aesthetic, slow motion.
